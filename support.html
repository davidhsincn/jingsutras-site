Support

帮助与联系

Questions, a typo in a text, or a problem with the app? Write to hello@jingsutras.com. We read every message and usually reply within two business days.

有问题、发现经文错字或 App 故障，请写信到 hello@jingsutras.com，一般两个工作日内回复。

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the sutra and section you were reading.

Common questions

Does Jing need an internet connection?
No. All texts and paintings are included in the app and work offline.

Where are the texts from?
The Chinese texts follow the traditional translations (for example Xuanzang’s Heart Sutra and Kumārajīva’s Diamond Sutra). The English is a new plain-language translation prepared for Jing with AI assistance and is still being reviewed; each sutra in the app lists its source. If you find an error, please tell us.

Can I read only Chinese or only English?
Yes. Tap the text button at the top of the reading screen to switch between Chinese & English, Chinese only, and English only. Jing also follows your iPhone’s text size setting.

Does Jing cost anything?
No. Version 1.0 is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is this medical or mental-health advice?
No. Sutra readings are spiritual reflection, not medical or mental-health advice. If you are in the US and are struggling or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), any time.
